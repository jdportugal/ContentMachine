## Descrição

President Trump surprised NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang with a phone call while Huang was on stage at the All-In Summit in Los Angeles, so Jensen put him on speakerphone for the entire room.

Trump used the call to reject the recent wave of AI doomer warnings, saying “the robots will not be taking over” and calling fears of AI taking over the world “a hoax.” He argued that slowing AI would help China and called data centers “the oil of the next 20–25 years.” 

Huang had already taken a similar stance on stage. He said AI safety concerns deserve attention, but argued that predictions of AI ending humanity are “not grounded in science.” He also said companies should slow down if they believe their own systems are getting out of control. 

The moment comes days after Dario Amodei, Sam Altman and Elon Musk backed calls for stronger AI safeguards, turning the debate over how fast AI should move into one of tech’s biggest fights right now.
