## Descrição

🇨🇳🐟 They swim like real fish. But they’re robots.

The BG-5 Golden Dragon Fish weighs about 3kg and is designed to move like a real fish, using a multi-joint tail instead of propellers.

It can swim autonomously at speeds of up to 0.6 m/s, dive to around 5 meters, avoid obstacles and plan its own route using onboard sensors.

Multiple units can also work together in formation, which could make them useful for patrol, inspection and surveillance in shallow or confined waters.

China has been working on biomimetic underwater robots for years, including robotic sharks. The goal is simple: make underwater machines quieter, harder to spot and better at moving through places where normal drones struggle.

This one was shown at the 2026 China International Fair for Trade in Services.
