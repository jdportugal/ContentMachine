## Descrição

🚨➡️ Comment "Guide" and we'll DM you the link!

Anthropic just released a free 37-minute guide on how to build and ship your first AI agent.

The walkthrough comes from Isabella He on Anthropic’s Applied AI team and focuses on the parts that matter once an agent needs to do real work: giving it tools, files, memory, persistent sessions, and a place to run. 

In the demo, they build an incident-response agent that checks logs, looks at recent code changes, finds the root cause of an outage, and recommends what to do next. Anthropic shows how the same setup can go further by letting agents fix code, open PRs, react to webhooks, and hand work off to subagents. 

The bigger point is that Anthropic now handles much of the hard infrastructure behind the agent, including hosting, scaling, session state, tool execution, and recovery, so developers can spend more time on what the agent should do. 

It’s free to watch and gives a solid look at how Anthropic thinks production AI agents should be built.
