## Descrição

📈🤖 Most people ask ChatGPT stock questions that are far too broad.

These 7 prompts give it a much better job: work out what the market may already be pricing in, read a chart, break down news, stress-test your portfolio, backtest a strategy, review past trades, and build a daily market plan.

Give it current data, earnings reports, filings, price history, or your own trades and it can help you dig much deeper. It can also flag weak assumptions, risks, and details you may have missed.
