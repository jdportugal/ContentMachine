## Descrição

What if Game of Thrones didn’t end and Westeros survived into 2048?

Someone used AI to answer it, and the world-building is genuinely inspired. The castles have grown into towering megacities. 

The great houses now command futuristic armies and advanced tech. Winterfell, King’s Landing, the whole map reimagined centuries into the future, but with the same brutal fight for the throne running underneath it all.

That’s the part that makes it work. It doesn’t just slap a sci-fi coat of paint on the show. It keeps the DNA, the houses, the rivalries, the endless hunger for power, and asks what all of it would look like after 500 more years of evolution.

This is what AI filmmaking is unlocking. Not just remaking familiar worlds, but pushing them into entirely new eras that would’ve cost a studio a fortune to even attempt.

The Iron Throne meets 2048, and the game stays exactly the same.
