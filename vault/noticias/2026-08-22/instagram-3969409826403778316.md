## Descrição

Comment "DEEPSEEK" to get DeepSeek Harness and build your own custom AI agent.

DeepSeek just launched their own free version of Claude Code.

It has already crossed 180k stars on GitHub in under a week.

It's called DeepSeek Harness.

Unlike Claude Code, it's fully customizable.

It's built on the idea that everything inside it is a plugin.

Including the model, the tools, the memory, and even the agent loop itself.

So you just swap out whatever piece you want.

You can build your own custom AI agent from scratch, piece by piece, exactly how you imagine it.

Which means you can run it with any AI model locally, without depending on one single provider.

You can even call Claude Code and Codex as sub agents inside it, letting different tools handle different parts of the job.

Plus it comes with 53 tools right out of the box, so you're not starting from an empty setup.

And it's completely free and open source.

Think of it like Lego blocks for AI agents, every single piece is swappable, removable, and replaceable.

Most tools lock you into their own model and their own logic, no matter what you actually need.

This one hands you the entire architecture and lets you rebuild it however you want, from the ground up.
