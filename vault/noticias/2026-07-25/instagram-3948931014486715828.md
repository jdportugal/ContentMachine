## Descrição

📄🤖 The hidden instruction was one line of white text on a white background, invisible on screen and readable to any chatbot it was pasted into.

Dr. Jason Gibson (@iamjasongibson), a history professor at Alcorn State University in Mississippi, buried it in the writing prompt for a discussion assignment on the Industrial Revolution.

It told the reader to place the word Madagascar in the response in a way that made no sense.

32 of 35 students across two of his classes did exactly that. Gibson said the country has nothing to do with the topic, and that the students clearly never reread their work.

Two contested the grade, and one partial failure was overturned.

The method is prompt injection. Language models follow whatever instructions they are handed, and this one caught the students who never read what came back.
