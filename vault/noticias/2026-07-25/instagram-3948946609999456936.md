## Descrição

🚨🇮🇳 India's cybercrime agency gave GitHub three hours to disable access to the source code of a messaging app that does not use the internet at all.

The notice, issued on July 23 by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre, names three Bitchat repositories and cites Section 79(3)(b) of the IT Act, 2000.

Bitchat is an open-source app created by Jack Dorsey. It passes messages between nearby phones over Bluetooth mesh networking, needing no mobile data, no SIM, no server, and no account.

The notice targets those properties, arguing the design blocks lawful interception and could be misused during internet shutdowns.

It lands during a youth-led protest movement in Delhi over exam paper leaks, where authorities reportedly suspended mobile internet. 

The Internet Freedom Foundation called the order "unconstitutional and authoritarian."

Copies of the repositories appeared on other hosts within hours.
