## Descrição

Harvard researchers found that students using an AI tutor learned more than 2X as much as students in a normal active-learning class, while spending less time on the lesson.

The interesting part isn’t just that AI helped, but it’s how they used it.

The tutor wasn’t there to instantly hand over the answer. It pushed students in the right direction, gave feedback when they got stuck, and helped them work through problems step by step.

Basically, it acted more like a tutor than a search engine. And that’s probably where a lot of people are still using AI wrong.

Instead of asking it to do everything for you, ask it to quiz you, challenge your answer, explain what you got wrong, or keep changing the explanation until it finally makes sense.

You can even tell it to make things harder as you improve.

AI is really good at giving answers. But it might be even more useful when you tell it not to.

What skill would you use AI to learn faster? 🤔💬
