## Descrição

AI just gave Paco a life of his own, a tiny chameleon at the center of a story that feels made for the big screen. 🦎🎬

Set across Arizona’s red landscapes, Paco is a lovable green chameleon with one strange problem: no matter what he tries, he can’t change his color.

His journey takes him through new places, strange situations, and unexpected encounters as he searches for somewhere he finally feels at home.

The film brings it all together with expressive characters, cinematic lighting, rich environments, and impressively consistent AI animation.

It also shows what AI is opening up for independent filmmakers, giving small creators the tools to build original characters, entire worlds, and full cinematic stories without a traditional animation studio.

At its heart, Paco is about finding where you belong without changing who you are. ❤️
