## Descrição

🐾🚁 When a beloved pet goes missing and darkness starts to fall, one New Jersey man sends a fleet of drones into the sky to help bring them home.

Michael Parziale, a 64 year old known as the "Drone Ranger," uses 18 drones, including models with thermal cameras, to scan woods, fields, and neighborhoods for animals that have wandered away from their families.

His team has already reunited 122 pets with their owners, from dogs and cats to one very unexpected runaway cow.

One of those rescues involved a puppy named Shade, who bolted into a freezing winter night and stayed lost for more than a day until Parziale found him in just 41 minutes.

Through his nonprofit, the USAR Drone Team, he works alongside his two sons and a group of volunteers, using donations to cover searches for families in need, especially elderly pet owners.

The rescues also support a bigger mission, because Parziale trains military veterans in drone skills that can lead to careers in surveying, agriculture, photography, and inspections.

With 122 animals safely home, he is proof that technology can carry a whole lot of heart.
