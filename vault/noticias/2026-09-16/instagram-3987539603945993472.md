## Descrição

GPT-6 Astra just had one of the most human AI moments yet in Minecraft. 😭⛏️

During a 14-hour run, Astra got further than any AI system Vals AI had tested. It built a semi-automatic blaze farm, collected six blaze rods, found a warped forest, killed more than six Endermen, and secured three Ender Pearls.

Then a creeper blew up its chests and bed.

After that, Astra seemed to lose all momentum. It spent the next several hours mostly farming potatoes, became fixated on avoiding creepers, and started criticizing its own mistakes in its notes.

At one point it wrote: “do NOT waste another night chasing dark pink pixels.”

The internet immediately fell in love with it. One of the biggest reactions was basically: “he just like me fr.”

AI agents are now getting frustrated in Minecraft and coping by farming potatoes. 😭

## Transcrição

GPT-6 Astra just had one of the most human AI moments yet in Minecraft. 😭⛏️

During a 14-hour run, Astra got further than any AI system Vals AI had tested. It built a semi-automatic blaze farm, collected six blaze rods, found a warped forest, killed more than six Endermen, and secured three Ender Pearls.

Then a creeper blew up its chests and bed.

After that, Astra seemed to lose all momentum. It spent the next several hours mostly farming potatoes, became fixated on avoiding creepers, and started criticizing its own mistakes in its notes.

At one point it wrote: “do NOT waste another night chasing dark pink pixels.”

The internet immediately fell in love with it. One of the biggest reactions was basically: “he just like me fr.”

AI agents are now getting frustrated in Minecraft and coping by farming potatoes. 😭

What are your thoughts on this? 🤔💬
