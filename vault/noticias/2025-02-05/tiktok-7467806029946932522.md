## Descrição

Comment ROSES to get this CHATGPT Framework. Want to become a ChatGPT expert in under 45 seconds? It’s easier than you think. Let’s break it down using the ROSES framework. First, Role. Tell ChatGPT who to be. Want it to act like a teacher? A chef? Just say so. This gives it direction. Next, Objective. What do you want? Be clear. Are you looking for ideas, solutions, or advice? The clearer you are, the better the response. Then, Scenario. Give some context. Share details about your situation. For example, if you're asking for marketing tips, mention your target audience and goals. Now, Expected Solution. Describe what you want the answer to look like. A list? A how-to guide? This helps shape the output. Finally, Seek. Encourage ChatGPT to ask questions before it answers. This ensures it gets all the details right. I recently tried this method. I asked ChatGPT for help with a project. It worked like a charm! By following these five steps, you can get better answers fast.
