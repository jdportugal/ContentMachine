## Descrição

As creative AI becomes more widely adopted by creators, the advantage is no longer just having access to the tools — it’s having something distinctive to say. The creators who break through aren’t just using creative AI to work faster, they’re using it to amplify their point of view. 

Adobe’s latest Creators’ Toolkit Report surveyed more than 16,000 creators globally to explore how creators are using creative AI to grow their business and audience, and transform how they work: 

87% say creative AI has accelerated the growth of their business and audiences 
75% describe creative AI as integrated or essential to how they work 
85% say the work they create with AI still reflects their unique voice 

Creative AI is doing more than changing how creators work. It’s also shaping how they think about what’s possible. 

According to Adobe’s latest Creators’ Toolkit Report, nearly half (48%) of  creators say that creative AI makes them feel more secure about their future as creators. In addition, 40% say that AI-assisted content consistently performs better.  Learn more on Adobe’s newsroom: https://news.adobe.com/
