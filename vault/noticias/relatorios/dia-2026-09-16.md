**AI agents burn 30-50% of their tokens just searching**
> AI agents burn 30-50% of their tokens just searching

Search is quietly eating your agent budget. Agentic search finds the right documents about twice as often as classical retrieval, but it costs roughly 100 to 1,000 times more per query and takes minutes instead of milliseconds. Agents are spending 30 to 50% of their total tokens on search alone, almost all of it up front, before the real task even starts. SID.ai's pitch is to stop making the main agent search: hand it to a sub-agent and train that model with reinforcement learning. The argument is that classic pipelines — rewrite, retrieve, rerank — bake every decision in at design time, so a reranker that knows the results are bad still can't act.
