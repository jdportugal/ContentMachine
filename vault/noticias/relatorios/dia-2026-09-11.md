**Anthropic catches Claude building rockets in Yemen and spying on 25M SIMs**
> Anthropic just dropped its new threat intelligence report, and the misuse cases are wild. A weapons cell in northern Yemen used Claude Code instead of hiring software engineers to write guidance code for a guided rocket and a ballistic missile with a stated range goal above 2,000 km. A consultant tied to Mali's intelligence service used Claude to monitor 25 million SIM cards, and Iranian security units scraped over 155,000 tweets to profile their own citizens. Russia-linked hackers ran AI agents that kept rebuilding malware every time antivirus caught it, and one scam ring ran dating apps where most of the "people" you'd match with were AI. The report covers everything Anthropic disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026, across seven categories, including Chinese AI labs trying to distill Claude into their own models. **The largest brain map ever, 166,700 neurons, is now free to download**
> Scientists just finished mapping a male fruit fly's entire central nervous system, and they gave the whole thing away. HHMI Janelia and Google Research published the connectome in Cell after a decade of work. You get 166,700 neurons and 125 million synapses, every one proofread by human experts, covering the brain, both optic lobes and the ventral nerve cord. It's the biggest brain map by neuron count to date, and you can explore it right now in Neuroglancer. And here is the crazy part: people are already dropping simulated versions of this brain into demos where it trades Bitcoin with $100, plays Doom, parallel parks and doomscrolls a fake FlyTok. Fair warning, some of those demos wrap trained or scripted controls around the neural model, but the activity still comes from the real fly wiring. **Claude Fable 5.1's full system prompt leaks within an hour of launch**
> Anthropic's newest model got its system prompt leaked about an hour after it shipped on September 1st. Jailbreak researcher Pliny published it as Claude-Fable-5.1.md in the public CL4R1T4S repo on GitHub, so you can read it right now. The core prompt runs around 180,000 characters and roughly 27,000 words, and some versions floating around with extra repo content hit 275,000 characters. That's up from about 120,000 characters for the Fable 5 leak back in June. Inside you get the exact tool schemas, search and citation rules, safety-classifier behavior, and the detailed rules for how Claude's memory feature files and applies what it learns about you. One thing to be clear on: this isn't a breach of weights, servers or user data, it's an extraction of the instructions the model sees at runtime.
