**A 166,691-neuron fly brain is now playing Doom and trading Bitcoin**
> A 166,691-neuron fly brain is now playing Doom and trading Bitcoin
The internet has turned a simulated fruit fly brain into the weirdest open-source toy of the year. Google and HHMI Janelia released a complete wiring map of the male fruit fly's brain and nerve cord, 166,691 neurons and around 125 million connections, built by using AI to stitch millions of tiny microscope images into a 3D diagram. Because the whole thing is public, people started plugging it into stuff. It's been dropped into Minecraft and Doom, taught to drive and parallel park, handed $100 to trade Bitcoin, trained on Beat Saber and a Rubik's Cube, and even put inside a Rabbit r1. Google's own AI account joked, "Oh, so this is why we mapped out all 166,000 of the male fruit fly's neurons."
