**DeepSeek's free Claude Code rival hits 180k stars in a week**
> DeepSeek Harness just made every AI agent piece swappable — free
DeepSeek just dropped a free, open-source rival to Claude Code — and it's the fastest-growing repo GitHub has ever seen. It's called DeepSeek Harness, and it crossed 180k stars in under a week, with roughly 50k in the first twelve hours alone. The whole thing is built on one idea: everything is a plugin — the model, the tools, the memory, even the agent loop itself. That means you can run it with any model, including local ones, and you're never locked into a single provider. You can even plug in Claude Code and Codex as sub-agents, and it ships with 53 tools out of the box under an MIT license. If you want the link, just comment harness and I'll send it to you.

**Humanoid robot runs 100m in 9.39s — faster than Bolt**
> A robot just beat Usain Bolt's 100m record by 0.19s
A robot just ran the 100 meters faster than any human ever has. At the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, X-Humanoid's TienKung Ultra won the race in 9.39 seconds — Usain Bolt's record is 9.58. Honor's robot, Lightning, had already clocked 9.32 in a test run, so this wasn't a one-off. And here's the crazy part: at last year's games, the very same TienKung Ultra needed 21.50 seconds for the same distance — that's more than twice as fast in twelve months. The five-day event at Beijing's National Speed Skating Oval has over 2,000 robots competing in sports and real-world tasks. If you want to see the race, just comment robot and I'll send it to you.

---

**Play games with your brain — 5-minute setup, no implant**
> A headset now turns brain signals into game controls in 5 minutes
Someone just played Black Myth: Wukong without touching a controller — using only their brain. At the World AI Conference in Shanghai, Stonehill Technology showed a wearable headset that reads your EEG signals and turns them into moves inside the game. It works by flashing targets on screen at different frequencies — when you focus on one, your brain produces a matching signal the headset picks up and converts into a command. No surgery, no implant, and a brand-new player is calibrated and playing in about 5 minutes. That's mind-control gaming moving from lab demos to something you just strap on. If you want the details, just comment wukong and I'll send it to you.

---

**1 in 20 AI medical notes carries a harmful error**
> AI clinical notes hide serious errors in 5% of cases
AI is writing medical notes in about a third of US practices right now — and the error rate should worry you. In the largest real-world study of AI-generated clinical notes, 1 in 20 contained an error serious enough to cause significant harm to a patient. Widen the lens and nearly 1 in 5 had an important omission, while more than 1 in 10 contained a hallucination — things like medications the patient never took, or symptoms that never made it into the record. The scary part is these notes read completely fine; the danger is what's missing, not what's written. And because there's no adverse event reporting for these systems, the errors just sit in patient records unseen. If you want the study, just comment notes and I'll send it to you.
