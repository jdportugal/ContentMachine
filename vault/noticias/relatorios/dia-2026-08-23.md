**DeepSeek Harness goes open source — free and fully hackable**
> DeepSeek's agent harness is free, MIT-licensed and fully swappable

DeepSeek just dropped its own agent harness, and it's free under an MIT license. Think Claude Code or Codex, except you can rip out the parts you don't like. Everything is a plugin — models, tools, skills, sessions, sandboxes, storage, even the agent loop and the UI. So you're not stuck with DeepSeek's models; you can plug in Opus, GPT, Kimi, whatever you want, and it ships with presets from a full coding agent down to a minimal two-tool setup. It cleared 95,000 GitHub stars in roughly two days. One warning: community plugins run code on your machine, so have a coding agent review them before you install. Comment harness and I'll send you the link.

**Grok Bot drops from $200 to $60 a month**
> Grok Bot just got 70% cheaper — $200 down to $60

Grok Bot used to cost $200 a month to touch. Now the cheapest way in is $60. xAI opened it up on August 21st to Cursor Pro+ at $60 a month, SuperGrok Plus at $100, and Cursor Teams Standard at $40 per seat — before that, you needed one of the $200 tiers. There's also a limited free trial if you just want to test it. The whole idea is multiple persistent agents instead of one chat window: separate threads for email, content, health, investments, each keeping its own history. That's a 70% cut on the entry price. Comment grokbot and I'll send you the link.

**Humanoid robots are scoring free kicks in Beijing**
> A humanoid robot just buried a free kick and hit the SIUUU

A humanoid robot scored a free kick at the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, then immediately pulled Cristiano Ronaldo's SIUUU celebration. The second edition runs August 22nd to 26th at the National Speed Skating Oval, with 2,056 robots from 666 teams across 16 countries — about four times the size of last year's first games. There are 32 events across nine disciplines: athletics, gymnastics, weightlifting, martial arts, tug of war and 5-a-side football. Underneath the fun, it's a public stress test of balance, coordination and autonomous movement. This year the robots dribble while moving and make diving saves. **Harvard's AI tutor beat its own classroom by 2x**
> An AI tutor doubled learning gains at Harvard, in less time

Harvard ran an AI tutor against its own physics class, and the tutor won. About 180 students in Physical Sciences 2 alternated each week between a highly refined active-learning session in class and a purpose-built AI tutor at home. The AI group learned roughly twice as much — and spent less time on the lesson. Here's the key part: the tutor was built to never just hand over the answer. It nudged students, gave feedback when they got stuck, and walked them through step by step, capped to a few sentences so nobody got overloaded. That's how you should be using it too — ask it to quiz you, not to finish it for you. Comment study and I'll send you the link.

**Apple's design rules are now a free Claude skill**
> A free Claude skill reviews your site like Apple's design team

Someone compressed Apple's Human Interface Guidelines into a Claude Code skill, and it's sitting on GitHub for free. You install it with a couple of commands in your terminal, then ask Claude to review your site. A few minutes later it comes back with every spacing, typography, color and motion issue that's off, checked against Apple's own guidelines, plus the exact fix for each one. It works on a fresh build or on a project you already shipped. So you stop guessing whether your design actually feels premium — you get a list instead. Comment apple and I'll send you the link.

**Star Wars rebuilt as a 1940s German war film**
