# NVIDIA pays $22,000 a year to host AI data centers at homes

> 0 item(s) · método: web · 2026-09-12T22:30:36+00:00

## Guiões

**Hook** NVIDIA is paying people $22,000 a year to host a mini AI data center in their backyard.

That number came from a viral post in late May, and the box is real. Here's the actual deal. A San Francisco startup called Span, the smart electrical panel company, announced a program called XFRA on April 13, 2026, with NVIDIA and homebuilder PulteGroup as launch partners. They install a liquid-cooled node outside your house, about the size of an AC condenser, packed with 16 NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs, four AMD EPYC CPUs and 3 terabytes of RAM. The node runs only on the electrical headroom your house isn't using, since the average home sits at about 40 percent of its panel capacity. In return, Span pays your entire electricity bill and your internet bill, and you pay Span one flat fee reported at around $150 a month. You also get a Span smart panel and a 15 kWh backup battery installed free. Nobody is wiring you $22,000. You're getting your two biggest utility bills replaced by one small fixed one. **Hook** You can now host a data center at home and get your power and internet paid for. Here's how to actually get in.

The program is called XFRA, from Span, backed by NVIDIA and PulteGroup. The first phase is a 100-home proof of concept in the Southwestern US, starting in the second half of 2026, roughly 1.2 megawatts total. Catch number one: it's new construction only. Span is working directly with builders, mostly new PulteGroup communities, so the node gets designed in before you move in. Retrofits for existing homes are being explored but aren't available yet. Catch number two: the official site at xfra.ai has a contact form, but the interest categories are Compute Offtaker, Investor, Supplier and Technology enthusiast. There's no homeowner button yet. If you want a shot, pick "Technology enthusiast," and if you're shopping for a Pulte home in Arizona or Nevada, ask the sales office directly whether the community is in the XFRA pilot. Broader rollout is planned for 2027, with Span targeting gigawatt scale. Comment "xfra" and I'll send you the sign-up link.

**Hook** NVIDIA will pay you $22,000 a year to keep an AI server next to your house. Here's what the viral posts leave out.

First, the money. No source from Span, NVIDIA or Pulte mentions $22,000 or any cash payout. The confirmed deal is bill coverage: Span pays your electricity and internet, you pay a flat fee of about $150 a month. One estimate for a high-cost state like California puts the real savings at $300 to $500 a month, so call it $3,600 to $6,000 a year, and far less in a cheap-power state. Second, the hardware. Sixteen 600-watt GPUs run 24/7, and Span's own spec sheet lists a 35,000 BTU heat pump for cooling and a noise level of 60 decibels. That's about a running dishwasher, permanently, outside your wall. Third, the target load is 7 to 12 kilowatts continuous, drawn from the spare capacity of a 200-amp panel, which is why the Span panel is mandatory and why your appliances get priority. Fourth, nobody has published the contract yet: minimum term, exit penalties and what happens at resale are all undisclosed. Still a decent deal if you're buying a new Pulte home anyway. Just don't quit your job for it. Comment "xfra" and I'll send you the spec sheet.
