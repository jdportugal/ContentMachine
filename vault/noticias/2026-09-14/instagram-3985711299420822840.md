## Descrição

Comment "GOOGLE" to get this Free Google Maps Scraper AI Tool. 

This free AI tool lets you scrape unlimited local leads without paying for anything.

It's called Google Maps Scraper Kit.

It's a free and open source scraper that runs on your own computer.

With Claude automating the whole thing for you.

You just tell it the city and the type of business you want to target.

And it comes back with a clean list of everything.

Their name, phone, email, website, and address.

Plus it even pulls their social media accounts straight from their website.

So you can reach them there too.

Think of it like sending an assistant out to walk every street in a city and write down every business detail for you.

No manual searching, no copy pasting from map listings one at a time.

No paying for a lead gen service that charges you per contact.

This works for cold outreach, local SEO research, or just building a database of businesses in any area you want to target.

Run it for one city or a hundred, the process stays exactly the same either way.
