## Descrição

AI companies are bulk-buying rare books, slicing off the spines for high-speed scanning, and shredding what remains.

The destruction is avoidable, since machines like the Treventus ScanRobot in this video digitize fragile books at 2,500 pages per hour without harming them, but shredding is cheaper at industrial scale.

And that's what has been happening. 404 Media reports that a service called ISBNdb brokered anonymous orders of up to 1 million books with NDAs included, and pre-2022 books sell at a premium because they predate AI-generated text.
