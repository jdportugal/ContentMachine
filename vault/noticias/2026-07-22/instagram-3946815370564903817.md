## Descrição

2026 is not about “using AI.”
It’s about understanding the AI stack.

Most people learn tools.
Very few learn systems.

Here’s what actually matters:

• Prompt Engineering — turning generic output into executable strategy
• AI Agents — systems that complete tasks, not just reply
• Workflow Automation — connecting tools so work runs without you
• Agentic Agents — planning, adapting, self-correcting
• Multimodal AI — text, image, audio, code in one flow
• RAG — grounding AI in your own data
• AEO / GEO — optimizing to appear inside AI-generated answers
• AI Tool Stacking — building one integrated system instead of scattered apps
• LLM Management — controlling cost, performance, and ROI

The shift is clear:

We’re moving from chatting with AI
to architecting AI-driven operations.

If AI becomes core infrastructure in your career or business,
these aren’t “nice to know” skills, they’re leverage.

Save this. Build the stack.
