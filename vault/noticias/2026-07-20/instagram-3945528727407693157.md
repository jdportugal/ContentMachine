## Descrição

Comment "PLUGIN" to get all four Claude Code plugins that fixes your usage limits.

If your Claude Code keeps hitting usage limits, here are 4 free tools that fix that for good.

First is Headroom. 

It quietly sits between you and your AI model, compressing and removing unnecessary stuff before it reaches Claude. 

Same results, using far fewer tokens.

Second is Graphify. 

It turns your entire codebase into a knowledge graph, linking every file and function together. 

So Claude doesn't need to re-read everything from scratch every single session.

Third is CodeBurn. 

It shows you exactly where your tokens are going. 

Broken down by cost, task, model, tool, and project, so you know precisely what to optimize.

Think of it like a bank statement for your AI spending. 

You finally see where the money actually goes.

And last is Ponytail. 

It optimizes your Claude Code output, cutting your usage by over 50% without losing any accuracy at all.

Together, these four attack the token problem from every angle. 

Compression, memory, visibility, and output efficiency, all stacked.
