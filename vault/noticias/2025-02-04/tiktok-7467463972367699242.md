Krea AI just launched a cool new feature. You can now turn images into 3D objects. And you can edit them in real time. It’s simple and fun.

Here’s how it works:

- Go to Krea AI and click on "Realtime."
- Upload an image of your object. Let’s say a sports car.

- Now, delete the original image. Move your 3D car around on the canvas.
