## Descrição

Dyson just entered the toothbrush market, and of course it made a $499 one with a camera and AI.

The new Dyson CameraJet uses a tiny camera inside the brush to take 28 images every second. An AI system trained on 470,000 dental images then finds the gaps between your teeth and fires a small jet of mouthwash directly into them while you brush.

It can also stream live footage from inside your mouth to the MyDyson app, where you can see your brushing coverage and check your teeth up close.

Dyson says it spent six years developing the CameraJet with 661 engineers. The result combines an electric toothbrush, water flosser and mouth camera in one device.

It costs $499.99 and is available now. Only Dyson could look at a toothbrush and decide it needed AI, Wi-Fi and a camera.
