## Descrição

The EU just gave ChatGPT an official new label: Very Large Online Search Engine. And the internet had plenty to say about it. 😭

The European Commission made the call under the Digital Services Act, making ChatGPT the first AI chatbot to enter this category. Reddit and Roblox were also designated at the same time, though as Very Large Online Platforms.

For ChatGPT, this means OpenAI now has four months to meet the extra rules that apply to the EU’s biggest online services. That includes regular checks of major risks, steps to cut those risks, more access for regulators and researchers, and tighter rules around how the service works.

The reason ChatGPT qualifies is its search feature. OpenAI reported about 159.1 million average monthly active users of ChatGPT Search in the EU, far above the 45 million mark that triggers the EU’s “very large” status.

So yes, Europe now officially has ChatGPT sitting in the same legal category as huge search services such as Google Search.
