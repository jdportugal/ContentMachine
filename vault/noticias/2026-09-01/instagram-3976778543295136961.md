## Descrição

🚨 Claude Fable 5.1 is cheaper, much stronger on several agentic benchmarks, more concise and, according to Anthropic, far less trigger-happy.

The TL;DR on cost:

$10/$50 per million input/output tokens → unchanged
Cache reads → 75% cheaper at $0.25
Typical workloads → ~25% cheaper
Agent-heavy workloads → up to ~45% cheaper

The biggest gains are on long, complex tasks. Fable 5.1 scored 55.8% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, up from 42.0% for Fable 5, and reached 73.4% on CursorBench.

Anthropic also put it directly against GPT-5.6 Sol. In its own tests, Fable 5.1 scored higher on agentic coding, scientific research, business workflows and knowledge work, including 55.8% vs 37.3% on Terminal-Bench and 73.4% vs 67.2% on CursorBench.

It comes with a 1 million token context window and can output up to 128,000 tokens in one response. Anthropic also cut cache-read prices by 75%, which it says makes typical Fable workloads around 25% cheaper and some agent-heavy coding tasks up to 45% cheaper than Fable 5.

Fable 5.1 is available today in Claude, Claude Code, the API, AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure.

Anthropic is clearly aiming beyond better answers here. Fable 5.1 is built to take on much longer pieces of real work with less supervision.

This is a pretty big jump for Claude.
