## Descrição

Scientists mapped a male fruit fly’s nervous system in huge detail, covering roughly 166,700 neurons and 125 million synapses, then released the data for anyone to use.

The internet immediately got weird with it.

People have since put versions of the fly brain into simulations where it trades Bitcoin with $100, plays Beat Saber and Doom, learns to drive, parallel parks, deploys databases, doomscrolls FlyTok and even changes its courtship behavior.

One important detail: the brain itself is not learning every one of these tasks from scratch. Some demos add trained or scripted controls around the neural model. 

The activity still comes from a simulation based on the real fly wiring.

And after seeing what everyone else was doing to it, one person decided to build the fly its own virtual heaven.
