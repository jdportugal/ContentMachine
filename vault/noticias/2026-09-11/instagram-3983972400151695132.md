## Descrição

This might be one of the best uses of an AI agent yet. 📰

@karenxcheng built an AI agent that creates and prints her own personalized newspaper every night while she sleeps.

She calls it “The Karen Times,” and every morning it gives her the things she would normally reach for her phone to check: her schedule, weather, to-do list, package updates, inbox highlights and curated news.

It even adds personalized comics and crosswords.

The idea is simple: wake up, grab the paper, and start the day without touching your phone.

Would you use this every morning? 🤔💬
