## Descrição

🚨 The cleanest training data left on Earth may be sitting in used bookstores, and AI labs are buying it by the pallet.

404 Media reported that ISBNdb, a book database company, now sources printed books in bulk for AI labs, in orders of 1,000 up to 1 million titles.

The value is in the publication date. Anything printed before 2022 predates chatbots, so it cannot contain AI generated text. Most of the open web now does, and training on machine written text risks degrading models.

Scanning at that scale destroys the book. The spine is sliced off so loose pages can run through a high speed scanner, then the original is pulped. 

ISBNdb offers an NDA on every engagement and never discloses the buyer, telling clients the optics are a real problem.

All of it is legal because of Anthropic. In June 2025, Judge William Alsup ruled that buying, cutting, scanning and discarding print books was fair use, since the digital file replaced the physical copy.
