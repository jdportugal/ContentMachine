## Descrição

👕📹 Berlin artist Simon Weckert (@simonweckert) created a shirt designed to stop AI camera systems from detecting the wearer as a person.

It’s called Digital Camouflage, and the strange pattern has a specific job.

Weckert developed it by repeatedly testing new patterns against YOLO, an open-source AI system that detects people and objects in video. Each version was changed until the system became less confident that a person was there.

He made the project as Berlin prepares to use AI video surveillance at Kottbusser Tor. The system there is meant to analyse footage and flag certain types of behaviour.

If his name sounds familiar, Weckert is also the artist who pulled 99 smartphones through an empty Berlin street in 2020 and caused Google Maps to show a fake traffic jam.

Now he’s testing what happens when the system watching the street can also be fooled.
