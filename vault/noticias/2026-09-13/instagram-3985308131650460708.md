## Descrição

SpaceXAI is about to try something pretty wild.

From September 15–17, a three-person team will livestream themselves trying to build an entire company from scratch using Grok Bot.

They’ll start with nothing but an idea, then work through the business plan, product decisions, engineering, deployment and other parts of running a company, with Grok Bot used throughout the process.

Grok Bot is SpaceXAI’s new system of AI agents that can use their own computers, work across apps and handle tasks from start to finish. 

SpaceXAI says teams already use it for things like sales, marketing, operations and coding.

The point of the three-day experiment is simple: see how much of building a real company can already be handed off to AI agents.

And they’re doing the whole thing live, so if it works, we’ll see it. If it falls apart, we’ll see that too.

Elon Musk has already shared the event, saying: “This will be cool.”
