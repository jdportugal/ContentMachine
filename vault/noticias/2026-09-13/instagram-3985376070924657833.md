## Descrição

In 1991, Terminator 2 showed two machines having a conversation through cloned human voices.

At the time, it was pure science fiction. The T-800 copies John Connor’s voice and asks a simple question about his dog. The T-1000, disguised as his foster mother, answers incorrectly.

That one answer is enough.

“Your foster parents are d*ad.”

It’s a small scene, but 35 years later it feels very different. Two machines imitate real people, exchange information and use a subtle detail to work out who is really on the other end.

Today, we have AI that can clone a voice from seconds of audio, impersonate people on calls and hold full conversations with other AI systems.

Which creates a problem Terminator 2 was already playing with in 1991: when a voice can be copied perfectly, how do you know who you’re talking to?

Back then, the answer was asking about the dog. In 2026, that question has become a lot more serious.

What are your thoughts on this? 🤔💬
