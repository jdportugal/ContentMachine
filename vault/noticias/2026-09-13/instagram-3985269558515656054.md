## Descrição

Naval Ravikant wrote “Everyone will soon have AI Anxiety” back in 2023, when ChatGPT had only been out for a few months.

Three years later, the past 24 hours made that post feel much more serious.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called on AI companies to slow the pace of their most powerful models so safety work has time to catch up. Sam Altman backed him, Elon Musk agreed, and Altman said OpenAI will not go public in 2026 because doing so right now would be “ill-advised” given the growing safety concerns around AI.

That follows a week where former OpenAI and Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon quit and warned about the race toward self-improving AI, while Anthropic published cases showing Claude being used for cyber attacks, spying and weapons work.

Back in 2023, “AI anxiety” sounded like worrying about keeping up with a new technology.

Now some of the people building the most powerful AI systems are openly saying the pace itself is becoming hard to manage.

Naval may have called this one very early.

What are your thoughts on this? 🤔💬
