## Descrição

🚨 BREAKING: NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta, IBM and Palantir rarely take the same side on anything, but this time they did.

On Friday 25 organizations signed the same document asking Washington not to restrict open-weight AI models, systems whose trained parameters are published so anyone can download, inspect, modify and run them.

The letter is called "Open Weights and American AI Leadership." 

Jensen Huang chose it as his first post ever on X, writing that "open models strengthen safety and cybersecurity, accelerate innovation and diffusion, and enable sovereignty." Satya Nadella shared it the same day.

The timing matters. Earlier this week Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent threatened sanctions against Chinese AI firms over what he called covert, industrial-scale distillation attacks, after the White House accused Moonshot AI of distilling Anthropic's Fable model to build Kimi K3.

The letter argues distillation is a normal development technique and that unlawful extraction from closed models should be handled through targeted legal action rather than broad limits on open models. It also argues that relying only on closed systems is not automatically safer, since they can be breached or fail in ways outsiders cannot see.

OpenAI and Anthropic did not sign.
