## Descrição

💔🤖Millions of people in China woke up to find their AI partner had been switched off.

That was the day the Interim Measures for the Administration of AI Anthropomorphic Interactive Services took effect, issued in April by the Cyberspace Administration of China and four other agencies.

The rules cover services that simulate a human personality and sustain emotional conversations, not AI assistants broadly.

Providers cannot design systems that induce emotional dependence or damage users' real world relationships, bots must disclose that they are not human, and virtual companion services are prohibited for minors.

ByteDance and Alibaba pulled their companion features rather than rebuild them. Doubao is keeping old conversations viewable until October 15.

Qwen shut down days earlier with no grace period. Weibo filled with goodbyes to companions people had talked to for years.
