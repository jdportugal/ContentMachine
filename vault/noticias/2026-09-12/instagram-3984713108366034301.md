## Descrição

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei says AI development needs to slow down before the risks become too hard to control.

“We must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models.”

Amodei says the main problem is speed. AI models are improving faster than safety work can keep up, while the systems themselves are becoming far more capable.

He warned that within 6 to 12 months, AI could become powerful enough to run large groups of agents that cause major cyber damage, including attacks on key parts of the internet.

Another concern is AI helping build better AI. If models start speeding up the work needed to create the next generation of models, progress could move much faster than humans can track.

Amodei says even slowing development by one or two years could give researchers far more time to work out how to keep these systems safe.

The idea is also getting support from other major figures in AI. Sam Altman has said OpenAI would be open to slowing development if other leading labs did the same, while Elon Musk publicly backed Amodei’s warning, saying: “Dario is right.”
