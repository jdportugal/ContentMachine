## Descrição

For the past few days, the internet has been finding increasingly strange things to do with a simulated fruit fly brain, and even Google is now in on the joke.

Google scientists build the complete map behind all of this: 166,691 neurons across the male fruit fly’s brain and central nervous system, with around 125 million connections. AI helped turn millions of tiny images into the detailed 3D wiring map, which the team then released for others to explore and use.

That decision has produced a very different kind of experiment over the past few days.

People have put the fly brain inside Minecraft and Doom, taught it to drive and parallel park, let it trade Bitcoin with $100, and trained it to play Beat Saber.

Now it has been put into an endless doomscrolling loop on an iPhone Duo, trained to solve a Rubik’s Cube, made to type Python, placed inside a Rabbit r1, taught YMCA poses and even trained to cut döner kebab with a knife.

Google AI itself joked, “Oh, so this is why we mapped out all 166,000 of the male fruit fly’s neurons,” before sharing several of the experiments.

One important detail: the brain model itself is not learning every task from scratch. Many of these demos add trained controls and other systems around its simulated neural activity.

Still, this has turned into one of the stranger experiments to come out of an open scientific dataset.

What should they make the fruit fly do next? 🤔💬
