## Descrição

Anthropic says a weapons cell in northern Yemen was using Claude Code across three missile programs at the same time.

That included a guided rocket, a long-range ballistic missile with a target range of more than 2,000 km, and a missile family called “R2000” that included a hypersonic glide vehicle.

The group used Claude Code for work that would normally need software engineers. It helped build the guidance, navigation and control software used to steer and stabilize the weapons, including integrating an open-source autopilot onto a phone-class flight computer, writing and tuning code, running builds and simulating flights.

They also ran several Claude instances at once. One handled coding, another did research, while a third reviewed the work.

Anthropic says it detected the operation, shut down the accounts and shared what it found with public and private sector partners.

The striking part is how much of the engineering work AI was able to take on.
