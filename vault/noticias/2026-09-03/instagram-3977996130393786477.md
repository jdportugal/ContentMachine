## Descrição

🤖🚫 New York City is taking a much harder line on AI in schools.

For the 2026–27 school year, public school students from preschool through 8th grade will be blocked from using student-facing generative AI tools, affecting close to 600,000 students.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani said the goal is to protect the part of learning where kids have to work things out themselves: “Kids need to learn to think for themselves.” 

He said students should still struggle with hard problems, ask better questions and learn what it feels like not to know the answer.

High school students will still get limited access to approved AI tools under teacher supervision, while teachers can keep using AI for tasks like lesson planning.

The policy will run for one year while the city studies what happens next.
