## Every Word Claude Writes Gets Marked

Anthropic just confirmed it: future Claude outputs will carry an invisible signature. Before you panic about your content, swipe.

---

## What the Watermark Actually Is

Not a visible stamp, but a statistical pattern embedded in word choices — detectable by machines, imperceptible to readers. The text reads exactly the same to a human eye.

---

## Why It Exists: The EU AI Act

The pattern is there because regulation now demands it: the EU AI Act requires machine-generated content to be identifiable. Anthropic is building compliance in rather than bolting it on later.

---

## What It Does Not Do

Compliance is the whole story — the watermark claims no ownership over what you create with Claude, and it does not track individual users. Your prompts and your published work remain yours.

---

## The SEO Question

Yours, yes — but now detectable, which is why marketers are nervous. Search engines have not announced penalties for watermarked text; what they rank remains quality and usefulness, not origin.

---

## The Originality Question

Quality is also the honest answer to the originality worry: a watermark reveals that a machine helped, not whether the thinking was yours. Ideas, structure and judgment still cannot be generated on demand.

---

## Transparency Is the New Default

That division of labour is exactly what this era makes visible: readers will increasingly know when a machine was involved. The writers who thrive will be the ones with nothing to hide.

---

## What To Do Now

So work as if every word is signed — because soon it will be. Use AI for drafts and scale, keep judgment and voice human, and let the watermark find nothing worth hiding. Follow for more field notes from the age of thinking machines.
