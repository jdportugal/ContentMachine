## Sixty Minutes to Read Claude's Mind

A frontier model shipped. Before the launch thread cooled, its private instructions were public.

---

## The Clock Started on September 1st

Sixty minutes is what separated the release of Claude Fable 5.1 from the publication of its full system prompt. Anthropic's newest model had barely been announced.

---

## Who Put It There

The publication came from a known jailbreak researcher, who dropped it as a single markdown file in a public GitHub repository. Anyone can open it right now.

---

## Open It and Keep Scrolling

Right now the core prompt runs to about 180,000 characters, roughly 20,000 words. Some versions in circulation stretch to 275,000, well beyond the Fable 5 leak of last June.

---

## What the Length Is Made Of

Those words hold the exact tool schemas, the search and citation rules, the safety classifier behaviour, and the detailed rules for memory. Including the files where the model learns who you are.

---

## What This Is Not

Learning who you are does not mean your data left the building. No weights, no servers, no user records were touched; this is an extraction of the instructions the model sees at runtime.

---

## Why Runtime Instructions Matter

Instructions seen at runtime are the closest thing to a published constitution for the machine's behaviour. Reading them shows you how it is asked to think, not only what it says.

---

How it is asked to think is now a document you can study. Comment prompt and we will send you the link.
