# RE/MAX Premium

The premium variant of the RE/MAX "Rede" system below, for listing reels.

- **Ground:** deep navy #001F44 (alt #002B5C). Photography carries the colour.
- **Signature:** a 3px red (#DE212B) hairline across the top of every frame, and a short red dash before labels and under headings.
- **Type:** Urbanist Light 300 headlines with the key words in Heavy 800. Labels are SemiBold uppercase, tracked +0.32em, in mist #C9D3E3. Refs, phone, AMI and scene numbers in DM Mono.
- **Frames:** photo frames, arches and outlines are fine white hairlines (45% white), never red.
- **Pills:** red pill with white text for the phone and status tags.
- **Scrims:** navy scrims over photos are the only gradients.
- **Agent close:** everything centred: role, portrait, red dash, name, agency, phone, logo and AMI.

# Real-Estate Design System — "Rede" (red · navy · royal blue)

A clean, trustworthy, portal-style system for real-estate agents' social posts. White dominates, navy anchors, royal blue guides, red is a signal. Inspired by the colour scheme of large Portuguese real-estate network sites. It contains no network logos or trademarks: drop in your own network logo where marked.

Sample posts: `Posts Sistema Rede.dc.html`.

---

## 1. Brand in one line
Clear like a listing page: white space, big upright headlines, rounded cards, a thin red stripe on top, and navy for the price and contact.

---

## 2. Colour
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Red | `#DE212B` | Top stripe, VENDIDO tag, date weekday, 1 CTA per post max |
| Navy | `#002B5C` | Price bar, primary buttons, dark backgrounds, headlines on white |
| Royal blue | `#013BA9` | Location + pin, eyebrows, number tiles, secondary buttons |
| Blue tint | `#E8EEF9` | Cards, list rows, photo placeholders |
| Line | `#D5DCE8` | Chip outlines, card outlines |
| Ink | `#1E2226` | Body headlines on white |
| Grey | `#5A6370` | Secondary text, legal line on white |
| Mist | `#C9D3E3` | Secondary text on navy |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main background |

### Rules
- Background split per post: about 70% white, 20% navy, 10% blue/red.
- Red is a signal, not a field. Never use it as a full background or for body text.
- Never put red text on navy or blue text on navy (too low contrast). On navy, text is white or mist.
- Don't use gradients.

---

## 3. Typography
**One font: Urbanist** (free on Google Fonts). Upright, never italic.

| Style | Weight | Case | Size (1080 px wide) | Line height | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | 800 | Sentence case | 120–136 px | 0.96 | −0.02em |
| Headline | 800 | Sentence case | 60–96 px | 1.04 | −0.01em |
| Price | 800 | — | 64–80 px | 1 | −0.01em |
| Body / subtitle | 500 | Sentence | 30–34 px | 1.4 | 0 |
| Location | 600, royal blue + pin | Title | 32 px | 1 | 0 |
| Eyebrow | 600, royal blue | UPPERCASE | 28 px | 1 | +0.12em |

| Legal line | 500 | As written | 16–18 px | 1 | 0 |

The headline is written like a listing title: **"Moradia T4 à venda em Viseu"**, not ALL CAPS.

## 4. Shapes and components
- **Red stripe:** 14 px full-width bar at the very top of every post (16 px on covers). It's the signature: always present, always on top.
- **Corner radius:** buttons and chips 12 px · cards and photos 20 px · story card 24 px. Nothing is sharp, and nothing is a full pill.
- **Primary button:** navy fill, white text, 12 px radius, padding 20 × 32.
- **Secondary button:** royal blue fill, white text.
- **Signal button:** red fill, white text. Use at most one per post.
- **Chip (feature):** white with a 2 px line-colour outline, 12 px radius, ink text, e.g. `4 quartos` `3 WC` `240 m²`.
- **Location:** outline pin icon (2 px stroke) plus the place name, in royal blue.
- **Price bar:** full-width navy bar, 150 px tall, at the bottom. Price in white on the left, agent name and phone on the right.
- **Number tile:** 84 px royal blue square, 12 px radius, white number. Used for lists and tips.
- **Date card:** white card, 20 px radius. Weekday in red caps, large day number, month in caps.
- **Avatar:** circle, no ring.
- **Photos:** inset by 56 px margins with 20 px radius, or full-bleed under the stripe.
- **Icons:** outline style only (Lucide or similar), 2 px stroke, in royal blue or white. Use them sparingly: pin, phone, share.
- **Never:** italics, slanted or diagonal shapes, drop shadows, gradients, emoji.

---

## 5. Imagery
- **Property photos:** bright, sharp, straight verticals. Prefer the façade or the main living space.
- **Portrait:** natural light or a plain light background, shoulders up, approachable.
- **Logo:** the agent's network logo in full colour on white. It goes in the header (posts) or the white footer (navy posts). Minimum height 70 px.

## 7. Copy (PT-PT)
- **Tone:** clear, informative, calm. Like a good listing, not like a shout.
- **Register:** formal (você). Examples: *Ligue*, *Marque a sua visita*, *Quer ser o próximo?*
- **Title formula:** `[Tipo] [Tipologia] à venda em [Cidade]`, e.g. "Apartamento T2 à venda em Braga".
- **Status labels (sentence case):** Novo imóvel · Vendido · Reservado · Baixou de preço · Casa aberta
- **Features:** as chips (`4 quartos`, `3 WC`, `240 m²`, `Jardim`), or as one line separated by " · " in stories.
- **Price:** `385.000 €`.
- **Phone:** `+351 900 000 000`.
- **Time:** `15h – 18h`. **Date card:** `SÁB / 17 / OUT`.
- **Required on every post:** the legal line with company name and AMI licence number.
- No emoji. No ALL-CAPS headlines (caps only for eyebrows and date labels).

## 8. Checklist before posting
- [ ] Red stripe at the top
- [ ] Urbanist only, upright; sentence-case headline
- [ ] Red used for 1–2 elements at most
- [ ] Location with pin in royal blue
- [ ] Price in navy (bar or text)
- [ ] Rounded corners: 12 px on buttons, 20 px on cards
- [ ] Network logo legible, minimum 70 px tall
- [ ] Legal line with AMI number
- [ ] No italics, diagonals, shadows, gradients or emoji
