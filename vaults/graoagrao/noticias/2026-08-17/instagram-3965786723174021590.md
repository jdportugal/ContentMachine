## Descrição

Comment "VIDEO" to try MiniMax H3, the new open-source AI video model.

China just released a free AI video model, and it's already topping the leaderboards.

It's called MiniMax H3.

It ranks number one in video editing with audio on the independent Artificial Analysis leaderboard.

It's one of the strongest open multimodal models out there right now.

You can use it to create videos from text, images, or existing video references.

It automatically adds audio, realistic speech, lip sync, and music that fits the scene.

All in one single generation.

And the best part, you can now run it locally on just 5 to 6GB of VRAM.

Thanks to an optimized version dropped on WanGP by deepbeepmeep.

That gets you 5 seconds of video at 124 frames.

Or use 8 to 9GB of VRAM for a full 15 seconds at 832x480 resolution.

It's a 1-click launch through Pinokio too, so setup takes almost no effort.

No terminal commands, no dependency hell, just install and generate.

Or if you want to try it online instead, you can do it through the official platform or on HuggingFace.

Either way, this is one of the most accessible high-end video models to run locally right now.
