## Descrição

Link Snacks CEO Troy Link says the business was bringing in more than $300 million in annual sales in the early 2000s.
Around that time, Link was set on nailing a unique and splashy national marketing campaign to tell customers that the family-owned Jack Link’s wasn’t just another “big corporate-America brand,” he says.
Along with Minneapolis-based ad agency Carmichael Lynch, Link Snacks built an ad campaign around brazen protagonists fueling up with #meat snacks before antagonizing an irritated-yet-intimidating Sasquatch alongside the tagline: “Feed your wild side.” The ads went viral on YouTube a year after the streaming platform launched, and the company started paying for them to air nationally in 2006.
