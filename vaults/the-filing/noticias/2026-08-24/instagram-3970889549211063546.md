## Descrição

Flock Safety’s CEO is asking for a little less surveillance panic and a little more compromise.

Over the weekend, Garrett Langley defended the company’s network of license plate-reading cameras and drones amid growing backlash over privacy concerns, alleged misuse by police, and communities pushing to remove the technology.

“I value my privacy, my family values their privacy, my employees value their privacy, but what we’re talking about is safety as well,” he told Fox News, “When people talk about just one of these things, privacy or safety, they’re prioritizing the wrong thing.”

Langley said Flock needs to do a better job listening to critics and finding common ground, while arguing that its technology can make communities safer.

Flock Safety’s CEO is asking for a little less surveillance panic and a little more compromise.
