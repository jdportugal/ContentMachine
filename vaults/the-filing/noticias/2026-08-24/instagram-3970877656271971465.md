## Descrição

KPMG’s Australian operation will let go of hundreds of workers and cut average partner pay by 13%, saying growth is likely to remain 'subdued' for at least two years following an audit scandal.⁠
⁠
The Big Four firm has been in turmoil for months after allegations that some of its most senior audit partners used confidential client documents to win new contracts.⁠
⁠
The revelation, alongside allegations that the firm mishandled a whistleblower complaint about the issue, has triggered a political crisis and the exit of most of its top executives in Australia.⁠
