## Descrição

Singapore has unveiled a package of measures to encourage families to have more children, offering parents more than $55,000 per child.⁠
⁠
Singapore, with a population of just over 6mn, registered a record-low fertility rate of 0.87 children per woman last year, down from 0.97 in 2024 and nearing South Korea’s world-lowest rate of 0.8. ⁠
⁠
Demographers believe a threshold of 2.1 is needed to maintain a stable population, absent net immigration.⁠
