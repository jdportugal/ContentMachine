## Descrição

President Donald Trump defended the ongoing buildout of AI data centers across the country and said the communities opposing them were making a mistake, as he continued to embrace the controversial buildout of AI infrastructure despite growing public backlash and opposition from governors from both parties.⁣
⁣
The president said he was letting AI companies “build their own power plants” and claimed these data centers were not taking “power from the grid,” which he described as old and “tired.”⁣
