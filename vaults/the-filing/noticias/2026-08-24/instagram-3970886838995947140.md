## Descrição

Dozens of IndyCars blazed through the heart of Washington, D.C. at triple-digit speeds as part of the Freedom 250 Grand Prix on Sunday, in another sporting spectacle for the Trump administration.⁠
⁠
To celebrate the 250th anniversary of American independence, President Trump hosted the Great American State Fair, a record-setting Fourth of July fireworks display and UFC fight on the south lawn of the White House earlier this year. The culmination of that celebration of the national semiquincentennial came when he waved the green flag Sunday to start the IndyCar race in downtown Washington.⁠
⁠
Earlier in the day, Trump took a hot lap around the track in the presidential motorcade before the race started as Air Force One, flanked by four jet fighters, streaked above in a military flyover. Accompanied by first lady Melania Trump, the president later retired to a box suite to take in the race.⁠
⁠
Elsewhere in D.C., the race required road closures and increased traffic.⁠
⁠
The openwheel event was organized by the Penske Corporation, which owns IndyCar, in partnership with the Trump-aligned group Freedom 250. A January executive order from Trump greenlighted the first-of-its-kind auto race.⁠
