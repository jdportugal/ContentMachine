## Descrição

The upkeep of Delhi’s iconic Lutyens’ bungalows is becoming increasingly expensive, with the annual expenditure on renovation, furnishing and repair of official residences of Union ministers touching a record ₹92.35 crore in 2025-26, according to data obtained by PTI through an RTI query.

The expenditure in 2025-26 was more than three times the ₹27.47 crore spent in 2014-15. Between 2014-15 and 2025-26, the government spent a cumulative ₹547.68 crore on renovation, furnishing, repair and maintenance of these residences through the Central Public Works Department (CPWD), which is responsible for executing the works, PTI reported.
