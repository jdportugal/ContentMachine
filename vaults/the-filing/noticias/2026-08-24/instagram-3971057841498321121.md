## Descrição

The US Department of State’s September 2026 #VisaBulletin has delivered a mixed picture for #GreenCard applicants. While most employment-based cutoff dates remained frozen compared with August, several family-sponsored categories moved forward sharply. For Indian applicants, however, the warning is clear: #EB1 could become unavailable before the end of the fiscal year, while #EB2 and #EB5 remain unavailable.
