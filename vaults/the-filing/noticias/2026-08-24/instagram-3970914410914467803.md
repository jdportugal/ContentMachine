## Descrição

Russian President Vladimir Putin on Monday said trade between India and Russia was growing again. He credited Prime Minister Narendra Modi for his "personal attention" to bilateral ties.

"I'm pleased to note that our trade turnover has increased this year. While it declined slightly last year, it's growing this year, and this is partly, and perhaps primarily, thanks to Prime Minister Modi, who is devoting his personal attention to developing Russian-Indian relations," Putin told External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar in Moscow.
