## Descrição

How do you go from streaming to an empty chat room with a cheap, second-hand eBay capture card to signing historical streaming deals and building a 25-person game development studio?

Welcome to the Forbes Top Creator Show! This week, host Steven Bertoni decodes the business of gaming and streaming with pioneer, creator, and entrepreneur Andre Rebelo—AKA Typical Gamer.

In this episode, Typical Gamer opens up about his 16-year journey in digital media, starting on Justin.tv in 2009 long before the word "streamer" was even a viable career path. He shares the emotional reality of dropping out of college twice—first for computer science and then for business marketing, after realizing his 80-year-old professor was teaching a textbook version of social media that Typical Gamer was actively living out in real time. TG reflects on his upbringing as the son of Portuguese immigrants, highlighting how his father went from growing up on a farm with pigs on the floor to seeing his son sign the first-ever exclusive YouTube gaming streaming contract in 2015.

Andre breaks down the highly strategic business behind Typical Gamer. He reveals the logistics of live-streaming his wedding to his fellow creator wife, Samara Redway (complete with a Fortnite drone show, "Forever Duo" vows, and her grandfather's hilarious "Gandalf" officiant slips). He also deep-dives into his game development company, JOGO, explaining why he refuses to take a single dollar of profit from the business so they can supercharge Fortnite UGC map building. Finally, he shares his three rules of creator success, his hot takes on Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6), and why short-form content is the only way for modern creators to escape the fatal cycle of streaming to an empty chat.
