## Descrição

Two oil supertankers were struck by projectiles while attempting to exit the Strait of Hormuz, maritime security consultant Marisks said. Separately, Iranian state TV cited President Masoud Pezeshkian as saying that the Islamic Republic will "take reciprocal action immediately" if the US "returns to its commitments" in the Memorandum of Understanding that was signed in June. Bloomberg's Abeer Abu Omar has the details.
