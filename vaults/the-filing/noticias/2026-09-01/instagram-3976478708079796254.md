## Descrição

London’s Underground has fallen behind its rivals in Paris and Berlin in recent years but not in the way some passengers might expect. ⁠
⁠
Rather than lagging in reliability or cost, the Tube has become an outlier because of the comparatively limited mobile coverage on its underground network. ⁠
⁠
That gap is finally set to close. ⁠
⁠
After pushing its expected completion date back from 2024 to 2026, Transport for London has promised that all of the Tube’s 121 subterranean stations and its tunnel system will have full 4G or 5G coverage by the end of this year, up from about 60% of the stations in June.⁠
