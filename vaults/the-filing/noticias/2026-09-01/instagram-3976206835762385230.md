## Descrição

What’s another crime for Mr. Santos?⁣
⁣
Adding to his lengthy list of punishments from all corners of the world, former New York congressman George Santos was fined $71,356 by prediction market Kalshi. Along with the fine, Santos was permanently banned from the platform, the first time Kalshi had ever brought down that kind of punishment.⁣
⁣
The penalties come as Kalshi alleges Santos used the app to trade on his own attendance at this year’s State of the Union address. Leading up to the event, Santos made several public statements on his possible attendance, attempting to influence the price of the Yes and No contracts. Those actions violated the company’s rules on trading on non-public information, Kalshi said.⁣
⁣
This isn’t the first time Santos has faced discipline for this scheme. In June, rival prediction market Polymarket, for which Santos has been working as an influencer, ended its relationship with him. And in July, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ordered Santos to pay $35,000 for trading on his State of the Union attendance.⁣
