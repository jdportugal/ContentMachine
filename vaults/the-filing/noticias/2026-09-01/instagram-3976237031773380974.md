## Descrição

Scottie is on the green.⁣
⁣
With his win at the Tour Championship at East Lake Country Club in Atlanta this weekend, Scottie Scheffler surpassed Tiger Woods to become the PGA Tour’s all-time earnings leader.⁣
⁣
Scheffler’s FedEx Cup title brought him a $10 million check, part of a larger $40 million purse. He's now earned $130,390,661 playing on the PGA Tour in his career, roughly $10 million more than Tiger Woods’ career total of $120,999,166.⁣
⁣
Scheffler is just 30 years old, nearly two decades younger than Woods. The reason he was able to blow by Tiger’s career earnings is simple: Golf has grown massively over the last twenty years, and golfers get paid a lot more money than they used to.⁣
⁣
While Scheffler's first tour win in 2022 earned him $1.4 million, Woods earned just $297,000 for his first win in 1996. Scheffler's first win at the Masters in 2022 earned him a $2.7 million check, compared to Woods’ $486,000 payday for his first major championship win in 1997.⁣
