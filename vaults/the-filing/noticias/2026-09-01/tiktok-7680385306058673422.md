## Descrição

Russian hackers are targeting travelers through HOTEL WI-FI. 👀
Microsoft says attackers have been compromising hotel and hospitality Wi-Fi networks to redirect travelers to fake login pages, steal Microsoft 365 credentials and tokens, and even deliver malware.
If you’re traveling here’s what you need to do :
📱 Use your cellular hotspot when possible
🔐 If you use public Wi-Fi, connect through a trusted VPN
🚨 Be VERY suspicious if the Wi-Fi suddenly asks you to log into Microsoft 365
🔎 Check where you’re being directed before entering credentials
❌ Never install a “browser,” “security,” or “system update” prompted by hotel Wi-Fi
🔑 Turn on strong, phishing-resistant MFA when available
Send this to someone who travels a lot
