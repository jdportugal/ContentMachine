## Descrição

Olivia Rodrigo's debut music festival, Daisy Chain Fields, raised $10 million for reproductive health nonprofits — and Melinda French Gates just matched it, bringing the total to $20 million. 

Chappell Roan, Stevie Nicks and Doechii were among the artists who performed for free. 

The funds will be split between 10 organizations, including Planned Parenthood and the National Women's Law Center, according to US Weekly.
