## Descrição

At least 933 workers are believed to be trapped at around half a dozen hydropower projects in Nepal following last week's floods, the country's disaster management authority said. Video released by Nepal authorities shows rescue teams at the Trishuli hydropower project in Nepal's Nuwakot district working to reach those stranded inside inundated tunnels.
