Saying Nvidia (NVDA) beat earnings last week "is a very serious understatement," says Ted Thatcher, who remains confident that the Mag 7 giant continues to serve as the tentpole to the AI trade. Ted then turns to his bull cases in Meta Platforms (META), Micron (MU), and SpaceX (SPCX).
