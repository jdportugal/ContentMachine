## Descrição

Tim Cook was no stranger to Apple’s earnings calls even before he was asked to temporarily hold down the CEO post for an ailing Steve Jobs.⁠
⁠
Still, the company’s first earnings call of 2009 was unconventional. Wall Street immediately addressed the elephant in the room with the first analyst question: Would Apple run differently under Cook, and was he ready to take the helm permanently if Jobs couldn’t return?⁠
⁠
Cook sidestepped the inquiry. “We don’t settle for anything less than excellence in every group in the company, and we have the self-honesty to admit when we’re wrong and the courage to change,” he responded. “And I think regardless of who is in what job, those values are so embedded in this company that Apple will do extremely well.”⁠
⁠
Those words would later become known as part of the “Cook Doctrine,” a blueprint for Apple’s values and strategy.⁠
⁠
Today, the numbers speak for themselves. Cook led Apple to become the first company to cross $1 trillion in market capitalization, then $2 trillion and $3 trillion. Since the beginning of his tenure, shares of Apple have rallied over 2,200%, the company has sold over 3 billion iPhones and it’s returned nearly $900 billion to shareholders in the form of stock buybacks.⁠
⁠
In his 28 years at Apple — 15 of those as CEO — Cook has outlasted the tenure of Jobs, the company’s defining co-founder. And while Cook has gone to great lengths to preserve the core values of Apple, the company he passes on to Apple’s hardware chief, John Ternus, next week is uniquely Cook’s own.⁠
⁠
Cook is a quiet and reserved man. When he does speak, his words carry a faint Southern twang from his upbringing in Alabama. A supply-chain mastermind, he is more likely to be touring manufacturing plants than spending time in Apple’s design studio.⁠
⁠
So it’s no surprise that Cook has borne constant criticism since the beginning of his CEO stint for lacking the charisma and vision of Jobs. Jobs himself described Cook as “not a product guy,” and skeptics have complained he plays it too safe.⁠
