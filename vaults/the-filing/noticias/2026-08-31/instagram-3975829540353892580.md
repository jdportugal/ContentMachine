## Descrição

A decade ago, Hallie Shoffner left a career in nonprofits to help her aging parents run their family farm in Arkansas. For more than a century, the land survived droughts, floods and market swings. But last year, soaring costs and vanishing profits made the business impossible to sustain. She sold off machinery and equipment, keeping only the land and the 1901 homestead built by her great-granduncle. “I couldn’t risk everything my parents had built,” she says.
 
Now, Shoffner’s story has propelled her into an unlikely role: Democratic nominee for the U.S. Senate. She’s among a growing wave of farmers-turned-candidates betting that economic pain in rural America can outweigh the conservative loyalties that have long defined the farm belt. Reuters spent three weeks with Shoffner and Arkansas voters examining an agricultural crisis — with farm debt projected to hit $625 billion this year — that could reshape the political map ahead of November’s elections.
