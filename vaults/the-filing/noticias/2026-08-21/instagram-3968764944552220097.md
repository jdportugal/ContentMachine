## Descrição

Israeli soldiers outside a besieged house in the West Bank village of Qusra were seen throwing belongings and other items off of a balcony. At a different home, Israeli soldiers were filmed throwing items into a well.
 
Israeli settlers began encircling homes in Qusra on August 9, in what rights groups say is an attempt to seize privately owned Palestinian land. The case has drawn international condemnation.
